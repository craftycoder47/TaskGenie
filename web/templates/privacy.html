Privacy Notice

Prototype notice: TaskGenie is currently being tested with a small number of users. This notice explains the information collected through this enquiry form.

What we collect

We collect your name, email address, business type, service area, services or products, and your stated 7-day goal when you submit an enquiry.

Why we collect it

We use these details to respond to your enquiry, prepare or improve the requested Growth Pack, and communicate with you about this product test where you have given consent.

What we do not want

Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, API keys, government identifiers, or other sensitive secrets through this form.

Storage and retention

During the prototype, enquiry records are stored in the TaskGenie application database. We will retain them only for as long as reasonably needed for the product test and related customer communication, then delete them as part of the retention process.

Sharing

We do not intend to sell enquiry information. Any future third-party service used to operate TaskGenie will be assessed before launch and reflected in an updated privacy notice where required.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about your enquiry or ask us to correct or delete it. For the prototype, contact the TaskGenie operator using the contact method provided to you after your enquiry.

Important

This prototype notice is not a substitute for a full production privacy policy. Before public launch, TaskGenie should establish the responsible data controller, contact details, lawful basis, retention period, processor/subprocessor details, and a documented process for access and deletion requests.
